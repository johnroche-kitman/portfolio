iP: Intelligence Platform

Prototypes

Two working prototypes, built on Material UI against the Kitman Labs playbook. Both read the same squad, the same principles library and the same set of Hudl clips, so a goal set in one is the goal reviewed in the other.

01

Planning · Schedule

Sessions: Video

The session page with a new Video tab. Hudl clips grouped by drill, a full uncut playback of every drill, and filters by athlete, principle and peak metric. Open a clip and the distance each athlete covered plots beside it, with a playhead that follows the video.

  • Video tab, grouped by drill, a clip per athlete who took part
  • Full drill playback for the whole team
  • Filter by athlete, principle, max speed, acceleration or heart rate
  • Distance covered per athlete, its playhead tracking the video
  • Development goals with the clips tagged from this session
Open prototype
02

Athletes

Individual Development Plans

A new area of the product. Every athlete in a table, filtered by squad; open one to see their season plan. Three sections, with Development goals built out: title, description, related principle, the tagged clips and a date-stamped coach’s commentary.

  • Athlete table, filtered by squad
  • Three development goals per athlete for the season
  • Every tagged clip, with the game or session it came from
  • Progress notes the coach keeps adding to
  • Coaches review and Player’s review listed, not built
Open prototype

Synthetic data throughout. Clips are static files standing in for the Hudl API: in the product the analyst tags and trims in Hudl, iP pulls the clips against the event, and those tags are what map a clip to a drill, an athlete, a principle and a development goal.
