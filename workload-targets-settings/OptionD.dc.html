How metrics are set up
Metric calculation lives in one place for the whole squad, not per day. Total distance is the average across the last 5 imports of a drill; accelerations is the total across the last 10.
Good at
Matches how the week is actually planned, so a coach reads it without training. Easy to see that one day is loaded heavier than another across every metric.
Tradeoff
Metric names repeat in every column, and adding a metric means it appears in six places at once. Reading one metric across the week is a horizontal scan of separate cards.
