What you measure, and how it is calculated

Each metric carries its own calculation: the value taken per drill or per session, whether that is an average, a total or a maximum, and how many past imports of the same drill feed the number.

3 · Targets
As many days as you like
One value per gameday marker

Training days are numbered relative to the match. GD-1 is the day before, GD+1 the day after. A metric can carry a target on some days and none on others.
